# 📖 EventGo User Manual

Welcome to EventGo! This manual provides step-by-step instructions on how to use the EventGo platform as a Customer and as an Administrator.

---

## 👤 1. Customer Guide

### 1.1 Creating an Account
1. Navigate to the EventGo home page and click **Register**.
2. Enter your full name, email address, and phone number (format: `2547XXXXXXXX`).
3. Create a secure password and click **Sign Up**.
4. You will be automatically logged in and redirected to the home page.

### 1.2 Browsing and Buying Tickets
1. Browse the events listed on the home page. You can use the search bar or filter by category (e.g., Music, Technology).
2. Click on an event card to view its details, venue, and available ticket types (Regular/VIP).
3. Select your desired ticket type and quantity, then click **Buy Tickets**.
4. Review your order and enter your Safaricom phone number.
5. Click **Pay via M-Pesa**. 
6. An M-Pesa prompt (STK Push) will appear on your phone. Enter your M-Pesa PIN to authorize the payment.
7. Keep the page open. The system will automatically verify your payment and redirect you to your digital ticket once successful. *(Please note: Payment confirmation may take up to 30 seconds).*

### 1.3 Viewing Your Tickets
1. Log in to your account and go to **My Tickets** from the navigation menu.
2. You will see a list of all your purchased tickets.
3. Each ticket contains a unique **QR Code** and a Reference Code (e.g., `TKT-A1B2C3`). This QR code will be scanned at the event entrance for validation.
4. You can also download your ticket as a PDF for offline access.

### 1.4 Resetting Your Password
If you forget your password:
1. On the Login page, click **Forgot Password?**
2. Enter your registered email address and click Submit.
3. Check your email inbox for a password reset link (valid for 15 minutes).
4. Click the link, enter your new password, and submit.

## 🛡️ 2. Administrator Guide

### 2.1 Admin Login (Two-Factor Authentication)
For security, the Admin Panel requires Two-Factor Authentication (2FA).
1. Navigate to the Admin Login page.
2. Enter the administrator email and password.
3. A 6-digit One-Time Password (OTP) will be sent to the administrator's email.
4. Check the email inbox, enter the 6-digit OTP on the verification page, and click **Verify** to access the Admin Dashboard.

### 2.2 Managing Events
1. On the Admin Dashboard, navigate to **Manage Events**.
2. Click **Create Event** to add a new event. Fill in the title, venue, date, and category.
3. Add Ticket Types (e.g., Regular, VIP) and set their prices and total quantities available.
4. You can also edit existing events, update ticket prices, or mark an event as "Cancelled".

### 2.3 Monitoring Sales & Generating Reports
1. Go to the **Dashboard** to see high-level metrics, including Total Revenue, Total Tickets Sold, and Recent Transactions.
2. Go to the **Reports** section to view a detailed breakdown of sales per event.
3. Click **Export to PDF** to download a branded report of the sales data (includes the EventGo logo and date).
4. The **Audit Logs** page tracks all critical system actions, showing who performed what action and when.
